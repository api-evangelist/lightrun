AI agents in finance can handle routine matching. The exceptions they leave behind are what hold up the close: GL postings that won’t reconcile, intercompany balances that won’t eliminate, and supplier invoices stuck between Ariba and SAP. Most of those exceptions start upstream of the ledger, in the systems around each transaction.
